## Your words are yours

Everything you write or speak into Snippets belongs to you. We claim **no ownership and no rights over your entries** beyond the narrow, technical permission needed to run the features you turn on: storing a copy so your other devices can fetch it, carrying a voice recording to a transcription service and back, delivering backup files to your own Google Drive. That permission ends where those features end — we do not sell your entries, use them for advertising, train AI on them, or use them for anything but carrying them where you pointed.

## What we promise, and what we can't

Snippets is free, and we work to keep it fast, private, and durable. But it is provided **as is**: we can't promise it will always be available, uninterrupted, or free of mistakes. What we can do is make sure a failure never traps your entries — they live on your device first, you can export them at any time, and the Drive backup can automatically keep readable copies in storage you own. Please use those.

To the fullest extent the law allows, our liability to you is limited to what you've paid us for the service — which today is nothing. Nothing in these terms takes away rights your local law says can't be waived.

## Encryption gives you the only key

Signing in encrypts your entries end-to-end: your passphrase and recovery code are the only ways to read your synced entries — that is the feature. If you lose both, neither we nor anyone else can unlock them, and we can't be responsible for that loss. ## Fair use

Snippets is a private notebook, and what you write in it is your business. What we ask is narrow: don't use the service to store or spread material that is unlawful to possess; don't attack the service or try to reach other people's entries; don't strain it with automated abuse. We don't read or moderate entries — but we may suspend an account that attacks the service, and we'll act on unlawful material where the law requires it.
